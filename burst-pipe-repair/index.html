Home / Services / Burst Pipe Repair

Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Lacey Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Lacey and surrounding areas. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate

What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.
