A dry house is not yet a finished house. After readings return to normal, the bare subfloor, cut-out drywall and missing floors still need attention. Rebuilding all of that is what water damage repair means. Our team in Lacey replaces what could not be saved and matches it to what was there before, so the room looks like a room again and not a job site.

No two repair jobs are the same size. A minor loss can need just a couple of feet of wallboard plus baseboard and a coat of paint. Why Water Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Rebuilding after a water loss differs from a normal remodel. Whoever rebuilds has to understand where the water travelled, what it affected and whether the framing inside the wall has fully dried. Because our crew has the drying-phase moisture logs, it knows where studs were wet, what subfloor received treatment and which areas deserve another check. Knowing the background stops issues from being hidden behind new wallboard and a coat of paint.

Matching is the other skill. A patch that shows is a repair you notice every day. Wall texture is blended, paint is feathered out to natural breaks, and flooring is sourced to line up with the boards already there when available. When a match is not possible, we lay out options, such as replacing flooring to a doorway. Our team pulls any permits the job needs and sends updates to your insurance company.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • Paint is bubbling, peeling or stained brown where water ran down the wall.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Laminate or vinyl plank edges are swollen, curled or separating at the seams.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Fix the source first, and only then patch or paint a water stain.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
